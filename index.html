Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DayFlow ("the app"), developed by Mayur Bobade ("we", "us", or "the developer"), handles information when you use the app.

1. Information and app data

DayFlow is designed as an offline-first productivity app and does not require you to create an account or sign in. Tasks, notes, habits, and focus-session records that you enter are intended to be stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive this content through a DayFlow-operated server.

Please note that this description must be revisited if a future version adds sync, account, analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or other network services.

2. Notifications

If you grant permission, DayFlow may schedule local notifications for reminders you configure, such as tasks or habits. You can manage or disable notification permissions in your device settings. The app does not need to send your reminder content to the developer to schedule local notifications.

3. Third-party services

The intended initial release does not use advertising or analytics services. The app may rely on platform services and software dependencies needed to build and operate on iOS. If the app's data practices or third-party services change, this policy will be updated to describe them.

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. In the intended offline-first version, productivity content you enter remains on your device and is not shared by the developer with third parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your productivity entries remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app. Uninstalling the app may delete locally stored app data. Device backups or operating-system features may handle app data according to your device settings and Apple's terms.

6. Security

Keeping data on your device reduces the need to transmit it to our servers, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure. Use an appropriate device passcode and keep your operating system up to date.

7. Children's privacy

DayFlow is a general-purpose productivity app and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to the developer, use the contact route below.

8. Your choices

You can choose whether to grant notification permission, edit or delete entries within the app, and remove the app from your device. If future versions introduce optional online features, the relevant choices will be described here.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL, with the updated date shown above.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer through the GitHub profile for Mayur Bobade. A dedicated support email should be added here before App Store submission.
